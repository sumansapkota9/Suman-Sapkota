Image format practice

Compare the sizes yourself: open the JPEG and the WebP in the Network tab and look at the Size column.

Surprise: for this photo the WebP (about 87 KB) is slightly bigger than the JPEG (about 85 KB). Picsum converts on the fly from an already-compressed JPEG, so there is little left to save. WebP wins when you export it yourself from the original image with a tool like Squoosh — always measure.
