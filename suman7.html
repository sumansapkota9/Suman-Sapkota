How to Learn a Simple Dance

What You Need

  • Comfortable clothes
  • A pair of comfortable shoes
  • Your favourite song
  • Enough space to move around
  • A phone or mirror to check your movements

Steps

  1. Play a song that makes you feel happy and energetic.
  2. Stretch your arms and legs before you start dancing.
  3. Be careful and make sure the floor is not slippery.
  4. Begin with simple steps and practise them slowly.
  5. Relax, follow the rhythm, and enjoy your dance!
