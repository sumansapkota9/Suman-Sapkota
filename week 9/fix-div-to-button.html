🖱️ Fix div to Button

A <div> that looks like a button is NOT a real button: keyboard users cannot tab to it, and screen readers do not announce it as a button. The fix is simple — use a real <button>.

❌ Before: a div pretending to be a button

Try pressing Tab — you cannot focus this div, and pressing Enter does nothing for keyboard users.

Click Me (div)

✅ After: a real button

Tab to it, press Enter or Space — it works for everyone, and screen readers announce "button".

What changed

Replaced <div onclick="..."> with <button onclick="...">. No extra JavaScript, no tabindex hack — the browser gives focus, keyboard support, and the correct role for free.
